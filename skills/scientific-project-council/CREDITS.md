# Credits and Project Origin

Scientific Project Council is a scientific and academic adaptation of [Claude Council Khalifa](https://github.com/faroukahmed89-droid/claude-council-khalifa), the business-idea council created by **Farouk Ahmed** ([faroukahmed89-droid](https://github.com/faroukahmed89-droid)).

The upstream project introduced a council of independent Believer, Skeptic, Investor, and Judge roles for evaluating business ideas. This fork applies the council approach to university, PBL, seminar, graduation, engineering, and research projects.

## Scientific adaptation

**[Essam-Elkholy](https://github.com/Essam-Elkholy)** maintains this adaptation. Its changes include:

- Five academic roles, adding a Domain Expert and Prior-Art Researcher and a Research Architect.
- Scientific contribution, feasibility, measurable experiments, baselines, and course-coverage assessment.
- APPROVE, REFINE, RESCOPE, RETHINK, and REJECT verdicts.
- Evidence provenance, independent sequential review, bounded quality checks, and evidence-based re-judging.
- Modular operating references, a session persistence helper, regression tests, and reproducible distribution checks.
